A workable maintenance plan

See the changes and understand the result

Where the platform allows, changes are prepared in a preview or working copy. The affected pages, links and enquiry route are checked before handover, including the visitor journey on mobile and desktop. You receive a record of changes and any unresolved issues.

For example, an illustrative repair project might replace outdated contact details, fix a broken enquiry link and check that the visitor reaches the right form. The handover would identify the pages changed and the checks completed. This is an example of possible work, rather than a client result.

Keep forms and connected tools purposeful

Forms, analytics and embedded tools can share visitor information with other services. Consider those connections when changing the site, and collect only useful enquiry information. Additional tracking or embedded tools need your approval.

Common questions

Can you maintain a website built by someone else?

The platform, hosting, access and condition determine what can be supported. Share the public URL first. Missing source files or unsupported components may need resolving before changes are possible.

Can you help with a broken enquiry form?

Yes. The work can trace the form through to its receiving service. With your permission, a safe test enquiry checks whether it reaches the intended destination, and any unresolved delivery issue is recorded.

Will we need to move hosting?

A hosting move is only proposed for a practical reason. Your existing hosting is the starting point, including the accounts, costs and website and email dependencies.

Can you promise better Google rankings?

Content, page titles, internal links and technical repairs can support search visibility. A ranking position cannot be guaranteed, and search performance depends on factors beyond a maintenance project.
