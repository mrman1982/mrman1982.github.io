Leave with a setup you can understand

The handover records the equipment and software configured, account ownership and key settings. It includes a folder-access map where relevant, backup coverage and the result of the recovery check. Practical notes explain routine tasks and who will look after them.

Acceptance checks follow the work being delivered. For shared storage, that means trying the intended staff access and checking restricted folders. For a server change, it means checking the applications and user tasks included in the project.

Know where your information goes

Common questions

Can you work with our existing equipment?

Yes, where it suits the applications, capacity and support needs. The starting point is what you already own. Any recommended replacement comes with an explanation before you approve a purchase.

Do we need an on-site server?

Not necessarily. A workstation, shared storage device or hosted service may be a better fit. The choice depends on the applications, connectivity, data and maintenance needs.

What does the recovery check tell us?

It shows whether the selected data could be restored in that check. It does not prove every file or application is recoverable. The handover records the coverage, result and any remaining limitations.
