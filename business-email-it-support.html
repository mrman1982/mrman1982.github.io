A working setup with a clear handover

Email work includes the relevant domain settings and sender authentication, which helps receiving systems check that messages are authorised for your domain. We agree a safe test message, then check sending, receiving and access on the devices in scope.

The handover records the accounts and settings changed, who owns them and any remaining limits. Staff guidance covers the tasks involved, such as signing in on a new device or using an extra address. You retain control of your business accounts.

Handle email access carefully

Mailboxes may contain confidential client or staff information. Access is limited to the task through a suitable process. Keep passwords, recovery codes and private messages out of the general enquiry form.

Common questions

Do we have to change email provider?

No. Account or configuration changes may resolve the issue. A migration is considered when there is a useful reason to move. Provider limits can affect old mail archives, contacts, calendars and device support, so transfer coverage is confirmed first.

Does changing email affect the website?

Website and email often use the same DNS settings, the domain records that direct each service. Email changes need to preserve the records used by the website and other services.

Will our emails reach every inbox?

No provider or setup can guarantee every inbox. Sending and receiving checks establish that the tested route works. Inbox placement also depends on message content, sender reputation and the recipient’s filters.
