Questions about IT and AI help in Kerry

Can you help with IT as well as AI in Kerry?

Yes. Gen AI Solutions is based in Tralee, Co. Kerry. Services cover websites, business email, servers and everyday IT alongside online and private AI, integrations, automation and staff training.

Can the work be done remotely?

Some website, email and software work can be carried out remotely with suitable access. Hardware work or a task involving the premises may need an on-site visit. The project review establishes the access, location and practical arrangements before work is quoted.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Describe the problem, the systems involved and your location. A public website address, approximate number of users or a plain-English AI task is helpful. Keep passwords, customer records and sensitive files out of the initial message.

Do I need a full system replacement or AI build?

No. You can start with a focused repair, review, setup or workshop. For an AI idea, a limited pilot can help establish whether the approach is useful before a wider rollout.

How do you handle access to our systems and information?

The work starts by identifying the access needed and who can approve it. Use appropriate account permissions and arrange a secure review process for sensitive material. For AI, the tool and processing location are considered before business information is connected.
