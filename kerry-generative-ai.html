The local problem

AI is useful, but public tools are not always suitable

Many teams in Kerry are already experimenting with ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Claude. That can be useful for low-risk work. The difficulty starts when staff want to paste in client files, legal documents, financial records, staff data, medical information, internal policies or meeting recordings.

A private AI approach starts by asking what should stay inside your business, what can safely use public tools and which first use case is worth testing.
