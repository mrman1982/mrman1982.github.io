Terms & Engagement

These notes explain how private AI assessments, training sessions and implementation projects are usually handled.

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Assessments and proposals

A first assessment is used to understand your business problem, privacy concerns and likely use case. Any paid project work will be agreed separately with a clear scope, deliverables, price and expected timeline.

Private AI projects

Offline, self-hosted and open-source AI projects depend on the use case, hardware, data sensitivity, staff needs and support requirements. Recommendations are made after reviewing those constraints.

Human review

AI outputs should be reviewed by a suitable person before they are used for client work, legal work, medical decisions, financial decisions or operational decisions that carry risk.

Privacy and sensitive data

Do not send sensitive files through the general contact form. If a project requires document, recording or system review, an agreed review process will be arranged first.

Compliance and legal advice

Gen AI Solutions can help design privacy-conscious AI workflows and staff policies, but this website does not provide legal advice. For GDPR, employment, sector-specific or contractual advice, speak with an appropriate professional adviser.

Support

Support can be agreed as part of a project or arranged afterwards. This may include troubleshooting, staff refreshers, updates and help expanding from one private AI use case to another.
