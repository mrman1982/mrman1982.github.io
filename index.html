The privacy problem

Public AI tools are useful. They are not always safe for client data.

Many businesses can use public AI tools for low-risk tasks. The difficulty starts when staff want to paste in client files, contracts, policies, financial records, medical information, legal documents, staff data or confidential meeting recordings.

Gen AI Solutions helps you keep the useful parts of AI while keeping sensitive material under your control. That may mean a fully offline setup, a self-hosted assistant on your own server, an open-source model, or clear staff rules for what can and cannot be shared.

FAQ

Questions Irish businesses usually ask first

Can this run fully offline?

Yes. Many document, chat and transcription workflows can run on your own laptop, workstation or server without sending data to a public AI platform.

What hardware do we need?

It depends on the task. Some pilots run on a modern laptop. Larger document search or transcription workflows may need a dedicated workstation or server.

Can you use open-source AI models?

Yes. Open-source AI models are often the right choice when you need privacy, local control and a setup you are not locked out of.

What data leaves our business?

For an offline or self-hosted setup, your files, prompts, meeting recordings and outputs can stay within your own environment.

Is this cheaper than cloud AI?

Sometimes, but cost is not the only reason to do it. Offline AI is usually chosen for privacy, control and predictable internal use. The assessment will help compare options.

Can you train our staff?

Yes. Training can cover safe AI use, prompt basics, sensitive data rules, private tools and practical workflows for your sector.

Can you support us after setup?

Yes. Support can include updates, staff refreshers, troubleshooting and help expanding from one use case to the next.

Can we start with one small use case?

That is the recommended approach. A small pilot is easier to review, easier to train on and safer than trying to automate everything at once.
