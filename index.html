Before we begin

A few practical questions

Help for businesses and organisations in Tralee, Co. Kerry and across Ireland.

Can you help with just one IT problem?

Yes. A website repair, an email setup or a review of shared storage can be a useful starting point. Describe the issue and we can agree whether it fits the service and what work is needed.

Can you help with online AI tools too?

Yes. Get help choosing and configuring suitable online AI tools, connecting agreed workflows and training staff. The choice depends on the task, data, account controls, costs and ongoing support.

Do we need new hardware for private AI?

It depends on the workflow, the amount of data and how many people will use it. Some pilots can run on an existing laptop or workstation. Larger setups may need dedicated hardware, which is assessed before you commit.

Can AI work without an internet connection?

Suitable document, chat and transcription workflows can run offline once installed and configured. We check the full workflow, including any integrations, before describing a setup as offline.

How are projects and support priced?

Work is quoted after reviewing the requirement and existing setup. Hardware, licences, hosting and any ongoing support are discussed separately where relevant, so you can understand the full commitment.
