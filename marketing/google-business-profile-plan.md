## Questions And Answers

Add useful Q&A if Google Business Profile allows owner-added questions.

### Do you work only in Kerry?

Gen AI Solutions is based in Tralee, Co. Kerry and serves businesses and organisations across Ireland.

### Do you offer GDPR compliance?

No. The work can support privacy-conscious AI decisions, safer staff practices and clearer internal AI rules, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee GDPR compliance.

### What is the first step?

The first step is usually a 20-minute AI privacy assessment to identify one practical use case and decide whether public, private, offline or open-source AI is suitable.

### What is the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit?

It is a practical review of current AI use, possible use cases, sensitive data risks, workflows and recommended next steps. Guide pricing starts from €495.

### Can you build AI that works offline?

Depending on the use case, AI assistants, document search and transcription workflows can be designed to run locally, offline, self-hosted or on controlled infrastructure.
