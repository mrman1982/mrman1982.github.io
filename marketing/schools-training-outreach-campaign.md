# Schools, Training Providers and Community Organisations Outreach Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Create practical conversations with schools, training providers, charities and community organisations that need safer AI guidance, private handling of internal documents and clear staff rules.

The primary conversion is a requested 20-minute AI privacy assessment.

The first paid offer is the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495 or an AI Safety and Staff Training session where that is a better fit.

## Positioning

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish organisations use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive staff, learner, student, parent, volunteer or board information to public cloud AI tools.

The campaign should feel useful and grounded. Do not present AI as a replacement for teachers, trainers, staff or professional judgement.

## Best-Fit Prospects

Prioritise:

- Training providers.
- Community education providers.
- Charities and local organisations.
- Schools with active digital learning or policy needs.
- Youth, care, family support and community services.
- Organisations with boards, committees, policies and meeting records.

## Buyer Concerns To Speak To

Staff may already be using public AI tools without shared rules.

Learner, student, parent, staff and volunteer information may be sensitive.

Policies, procedures and board documents can be hard to search.

Meeting notes may contain sensitive internal information.

The organisation may need plain-English training rather than a technical build.

## Offer Angles

### AI Safety and Staff Training

Best when the organisation needs shared rules, examples and confidence around what can and cannot go into public AI tools.

### AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

Best when the organisation wants to review possible AI use cases, sensitive data risks, staff workflows and the right next step. Guide pricing starts from €495.

### Private Document Assistant

Best for approved policies, procedures, internal handbooks, training materials, board packs or operational documents.

### Offline Meeting Intelligence

Best for boards, management teams and internal meetings where notes and action points are useful but public upload is not appropriate.

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

- Are staff already using AI tools for lesson planning, admin, summaries, policies or communications?
- Is there clear guidance on what data should not be pasted into AI tools?
- Are there policies or procedures staff need to search more easily?
- Are meeting notes or action points a recurring pain point?
- Would training, an audit or a small private setup be the most useful first step?
- Who needs to approve the next step?

## Suggested Training Topics

Keep training practical and scenario-led:

- What public AI tools are useful for.
- What not to paste into AI tools.
- How to redact or generalise prompts.
- How to review AI output.
- When private AI is more suitable.
- How to create a simple internal AI use policy.
- Example prompts for low-risk work.

## Objections

### We do not have budget for a big AI project

The first step does not need to be a big project. It can be a short staff session or an AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495.

### We are worried about student or learner data

That is the right concern. The work starts by identifying what information should stay out of public tools and whether any private or offline workflows are appropriate.

### We already have policies

Policies are useful only if staff can apply them in real situations. Training can turn policy into clear examples.

### We need GDPR compliance

This work can support safer practices and clearer AI use, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee GDPR compliance.

## Qualification Checklist

Good fit:

- Staff are already using AI or asking about it.
- Sensitive learner, student, staff, volunteer or board information is involved.
- There is a need for plain-English training or policy support.
- The organisation has repeated documents, policies or meeting workflows.

Poor fit:

- Wants AI to replace professional judgement.
- Wants free general awareness only.
- Wants to upload sensitive material casually.
- Wants guaranteed compliance.
