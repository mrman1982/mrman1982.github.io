# Sales Call Script

## Purpose

Use this script for the 20-minute AI privacy assessment and early discovery calls.

The call should qualify the enquiry, identify one practical AI use case and recommend the safest next step.

The call should not ask the prospect to send sensitive files through email or a general contact form.

## Positioning To Hold

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive business data to public cloud AI tools.

The focus is private, offline, self-hosted and open-source AI for organisations that handle sensitive documents, meetings and internal knowledge.

## 20-Minute Call Structure

### Minute 0 to 2: Set The Frame

Suggested opening:

Thanks for taking the time. The aim of this call is to identify one practical AI use case, understand whether sensitive data is involved and decide whether public, private, offline or open-source AI is the right direction.

We will keep this high-level today. Please do not share client files, confidential documents or sensitive records on this call. If anything needs secure review later, we can agree a safer process.

### Minute 2 to 6: Understand The Organisation

Questions:

- What type of organisation is this?
- How many people would potentially use AI?
- What kind of sensitive information do you handle?
- Are staff already using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Claude?
- Is there any internal guidance on AI use today?

Listen for:

- Client files.
- Legal documents.
- Financial records.
- Staff data.
- Learner, student or parent information.
- Medical, care or service user data.
- Meeting recordings.
- Internal policies and procedures.

### Minute 6 to 12: Find One Practical Use Case

Questions:

- What workflow made you interested in AI?
- Where do staff lose time looking for information?
- Are meetings, notes or action points a recurring issue?
- Are internal documents hard to search?
- Are staff asking for permission to use AI?
- Is the main concern privacy, accuracy, cost, staff misuse or knowing where to start?

Map the answer to one offer:

- Unsure where to start: AI Privacy and Readiness Audit.
- Staff using AI without rules: AI Safety and Staff Training.
- Hard-to-search documents: Private Document Assistant.
- Meeting recordings or notes: Offline Meeting Intelligence.
- Need local AI control: Offline AI Starter Setup.

### Minute 12 to 16: Privacy And Fit

Questions:

- Would this use case involve sensitive data?
- Could it be tested with sample or redacted data first?
- Does the data need to stay on your own hardware or controlled infrastructure?
- Who would approve the first step?
- Is there a budget range for an initial audit or pilot?

Clarifying statement:

Private or offline AI is not automatically the right answer for every use case. The best option depends on the sensitivity of the data, the workflow, hardware, scale and support requirements.

### Minute 16 to 19: Recommend The Next Step

Use one of these recommendations.

#### AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

Based on what you have described, I would start with the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit. It starts from €495 and reviews the use case, sensitive data risks, current workflow, suitable tool options and practical next steps. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee compliance, but it gives you a clear action plan before spending money on a build.

#### AI Safety and Staff Training

It sounds like the immediate need is staff guidance. I would suggest a practical AI safety session that covers what can and cannot go into public tools, safer prompting, human review and when private AI is more appropriate.

#### Private Document Assistant

The strongest use case sounds like internal document search. The next step would be to scope a Private Document Assistant using approved documents, with source references and a clear decision on where the data is processed.

#### Offline Meeting Intelligence

Your meeting workflow sounds like the right starting point. The next step would be to scope Offline Meeting Intelligence for transcription, summaries, action points and searchable records without default public upload.

#### Offline AI Starter Setup

It sounds like you want a controlled AI starting point for a small team. The next step would be an Offline AI Starter Setup, likely after a short audit to confirm hardware, data and support requirements.

### Minute 19 to 20: Close

Suggested close:

The next step from here is that I will send a short summary and, if appropriate, an outline for the audit or service we discussed. Please do not send sensitive files by email at this stage. If document review is needed, we will agree a safer process first.

Would you like me to send that through?

## Qualification Score

Use a simple score from 1 to 5.

### Score 5

Clear sensitive-data use case, decision-maker present, budget for audit, practical urgency, good fit for privacy-first AI.

### Score 4

Good use case and clear concern, but decision process or timing needs work.

### Score 3

Interested but unclear use case. Offer the audit if they value structure.

### Score 2

Generic curiosity with no problem, budget or decision-maker.

### Score 1

Poor fit, wants hype, guaranteed compliance, speculative automation or free advice only.

## Discovery Question Bank

### Current AI Use

- What AI tools are staff using now?
- Are they using personal or business accounts?
- Who approves new AI tools?
- Have you had any concerns about staff pasting sensitive information into AI tools?

### Data Sensitivity

- What types of sensitive data are involved?
- Does the data need to stay on your own hardware or controlled infrastructure?
- Are there documents or recordings that should never be uploaded to public tools?
- Are there low-risk tasks where public AI is acceptable?

### Documents

- What documents do staff search most often?
- Are policies, procedures or templates stored in one place?
- Do staff ask the same internal questions repeatedly?
- Would source references matter?

### Meetings

- Are meetings recorded?
- Who writes notes and action points?
- Do recordings contain sensitive information?
- Would searchable meeting records be useful?

### Staff Training

- Do staff know what data should not go into public AI tools?
- Do staff know how to review AI output?
- Is there a written AI usage policy?
- Who needs training first?

### Decision

- Who needs to approve the first step?
- Is there a budget for an audit or pilot?
- Is there a deadline or event driving this?
- What would make this feel safe enough to test?

## Objection Handling

### We need GDPR compliance

I understand. This work can support privacy-conscious AI decisions, safer staff practices and clearer internal rules, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee GDPR compliance. For legal advice, you should speak with an appropriate adviser.

### We are already using Copilot

That may be suitable for some work. The question is which data and workflows fit that environment, which need extra controls and which may need a private or offline option.

### We are too small for AI

Small teams often benefit from starting with one narrow use case. The audit starts from €495 and is designed to avoid overbuilding.

### We do not want to upload documents

That is a sensible concern. We can start with a high-level review and use sample or redacted material for demos. If sensitive material needs review later, we agree a safer process first.

### Can you guarantee this will save time?

No. I do not promise unsupported savings. The aim is to choose a practical use case, test it properly and decide whether it is worth expanding.

### Can AI make decisions for us?

For sensitive business, legal, medical, financial or operational work, AI output should be reviewed by a person. The focus here is support for search, summaries, drafting and internal knowledge, not blind automation.
