# Accountants and Bookkeepers Outreach Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Start conversations with Irish accountants and bookkeepers who are curious about AI but cautious about client data, payroll information, tax records and financial documents.

The primary conversion is a requested 20-minute AI privacy assessment.

The primary paid offer is the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495.

## Positioning

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish accountancy and bookkeeping teams use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive client data to public cloud AI tools.

This is not a generic AI automation offer. The angle is safer AI use, private document search, offline meeting notes and practical internal workflows.

## Best-Fit Prospects

Prioritise:

- Small and mid-sized accountancy practices.
- Bookkeeping firms.
- Payroll bureaux.
- Tax advisers.
- Local professional services firms that handle client files.

Look for signals:

- Multiple staff.
- Publicly listed services such as payroll, VAT, accounts preparation or tax returns.
- Content about digital transformation, cloud accounting or advisory services.
- Owners or partners active on LinkedIn.

## Buyer Concerns To Speak To

Staff may already be using ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini without clear rules.

Client files, payroll records, tax details and financial statements should not be casually pasted into public AI tools.

Internal knowledge can be hard to find across templates, checklists, policies and procedures.

Meeting notes and client calls can contain sensitive information.

The firm may want practical AI help without buying enterprise software or starting a large project.

## Offer Angles

### AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

Best for firms that are unsure where to start. The audit reviews current AI use, privacy risks, suitable workflows and a practical next step. Guide pricing starts from €495.

### Private Document Assistant

Best for firms with internal procedures, tax checklists, onboarding documents, workflow notes or staff guidance that people need to search quickly.

### AI Safety and Staff Training

Best for firms where staff are experimenting with public AI tools and management wants clearer rules.

### Offline Meeting Intelligence

Best for firms that want private notes and action points from internal meetings or client calls without uploading recordings to public platforms.

## Prospect List Sources

Use only lawful, relevant, publicly available business contact sources.

- Firm websites.
- LinkedIn company pages.
- LinkedIn profiles for partners, directors and practice managers.
- Local chamber directories.
- Business networks in Kerry, Cork, Limerick, Galway and Dublin.
- Accounting software partner directories where contact details are public.

Do not collect unnecessary personal data. Keep the tracker focused on business outreach.

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

- Are staff currently using ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Claude?
- Do you have any internal rules for what can and cannot be pasted into AI tools?
- Which documents or workflows are hardest to search or reuse?
- Do you record or summarise internal meetings or client calls?
- Is the main concern privacy, staff misuse, cost, accuracy or knowing where to start?
- Would a small audit be useful before considering any build work?

Avoid asking for client files during the first conversation.

## Objections

### We already use Microsoft Copilot

That may be the right tool for some tasks. The audit is not about replacing tools automatically. It is about deciding what data and workflows are suitable for public, enterprise, private or offline AI.

### We are too small for this

That is why the first step is small. The audit starts from €495 and focuses on one or two practical use cases, not a large transformation project.

### We need GDPR compliance

The audit can support privacy-conscious decisions, data classification and safer staff practices, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee GDPR compliance.

### We do not want AI making decisions

That is sensible. The recommended use cases usually focus on search, drafting support, summaries and staff guidance, with human review.

## Qualification Checklist

Good fit:

- Handles sensitive client data.
- Has multiple staff or repeated workflows.
- Is already experimenting with AI or considering it.
- Wants practical advice before buying tools.
- Can pay for an audit or small setup.

Poor fit:

- Wants generic AI content generation only.
- Wants guaranteed compliance.
- Wants to upload sensitive files casually.
- Wants a speculative build with no clear business use case.
