# Demo Video Plan

## Goal

Create short demo videos that make the offer tangible without inventing case studies or using sensitive client data.

Each video should lead to one action:

Request a 20-minute AI privacy assessment.

## Demo Rules

Use sample documents, synthetic meeting recordings or internal demo material only.

Label every video clearly as a demo or example build.

Do not imply that the demo is a client result.

Do not use client logos, private documents, real meeting recordings or real confidential material.

Do not claim GDPR compliance or guaranteed outcomes.

Keep each video between 90 seconds and 4 minutes.

## Demo 1: Private Document Assistant

### Purpose

Show how a business can ask questions from approved documents without uploading sensitive files to public AI tools.

### Sample Material

Use made-up documents such as:

- Staff AI usage policy.
- Sample onboarding procedure.
- Example health and safety policy.
- Sample internal finance procedure.

### Storyboard

Open with the problem:

"Many businesses have policies, procedures and internal documents, but staff still have to search manually or ask the same questions repeatedly."

Show the assistant:

- Ask a question.
- Show an answer.
- Show source references.
- Explain that the document set is approved and controlled.

Privacy point:

"A real version can be designed for local, self-hosted or controlled infrastructure where appropriate."

CTA:

"If this would help your team, request a 20-minute AI privacy assessment."

### Suggested Questions To Demo

- What should staff avoid putting into public AI tools?
- What is the process for requesting approval?
- Where does the policy mention human review?
- What are the steps in the onboarding procedure?

## Production Checklist

Before recording:

- Confirm all demo data is synthetic or non-sensitive.
- Add "Demo project" or "Example build" on screen.
- Prepare the CTA.
- Keep the script plain-English.
- Avoid generic AI claims.

During recording:

- Show the real workflow, not slides only.
- Speak calmly.
- Explain privacy trade-offs carefully.
- Keep the focus on one use case.

After recording:

- Add captions.
- Add a short description.
- Link to the relevant page or contact form.
- Post on LinkedIn and Google Business Profile.
- Use in direct outreach only where relevant.
