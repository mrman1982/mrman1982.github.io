# Solicitors and Legal Offices Outreach Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Start careful, trust-based conversations with Irish solicitors and legal offices about safer AI use, private document search and internal AI rules.

The primary conversion is a requested 20-minute AI privacy assessment.

The first paid offer is the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495.

## Positioning

Gen AI Solutions helps legal offices explore AI privately and practically, without sending confidential client information to public cloud AI tools.

The campaign must not imply legal advice, guaranteed compliance or automated legal judgement. The focus is practical AI support, human review, privacy-conscious workflows and internal controls.

## Best-Fit Prospects

Prioritise:

- Small and mid-sized solicitors' practices.
- Legal offices with several fee earners or support staff.
- Practices with document-heavy work.
- Firms that publish updates, resources or service pages.
- Local firms in Kerry, Cork, Limerick, Galway, Waterford and Dublin.

## Buyer Concerns To Speak To

Client confidentiality matters.

Staff may already be experimenting with public AI tools without a clear policy.

Legal documents, contracts, correspondence and case notes should not be casually uploaded into public AI tools.

Internal templates, precedents, checklists and policies can be hard to search.

AI output must be reviewed by a person and should not be treated as legal advice.

## Offer Angles

### AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

The safest first step. It reviews current AI use, sensitive data risks, workflows, staff rules and practical next steps. Guide pricing starts from €495.

### Private Document Assistant

Useful where staff need to search approved internal documents, policies, templates, procedures or knowledge notes. It can be designed to show source references.

### AI Safety and Staff Training

Useful where partners want staff to understand what should not be pasted into public tools, how to review outputs and when private AI is more appropriate.

### Offline Meeting Intelligence

Useful for private transcription and summaries of internal meetings, interviews or notes where public upload is not suitable.

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

- Are staff currently using public AI tools for drafting, summaries or research support?
- Do you have a written AI usage policy?
- Which internal documents or templates are hardest to find or reuse?
- Would private document search be useful if it only used approved internal files?
- Are there meetings, interviews or notes that would benefit from private transcription?
- What would make AI feel safe enough to test in a limited way?

Do not ask for client files on the first call.

## Suggested 20-Minute Assessment Flow

First five minutes:

Confirm the office's interest in AI and current concerns.

Middle ten minutes:

Identify one use case and whether sensitive information is involved.

Final five minutes:

Recommend one next step. That may be an audit, staff training, a private document assistant demo, or no build yet.

## Objections

### We cannot use AI because of confidentiality

That caution is reasonable. The conversation is about whether any AI use can be done safely with clear limits, local tools, approved documents and human review.

### We already have internal policies

That is a good starting point. The audit can review whether staff guidance maps onto actual workflows and whether private AI has a role.

### We need compliance certainty

The work can support safer practices and privacy-conscious AI design, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee compliance.

### We do not want automated legal advice

Neither do we. The recommended use cases should support search, drafting, summarising and internal knowledge retrieval with human review.
