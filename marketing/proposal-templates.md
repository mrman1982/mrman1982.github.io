# Proposal Templates

## How To Use These Templates

These are starting points for proposals after a 20-minute AI privacy assessment.

Keep each proposal specific to the client's actual use case. Do not over-sell. Do not add compliance promises. Do not invent proof.

Every proposal should include:

- The business problem.
- The sensitive data concern.
- The recommended first step.
- What is included.
- What is not included.
- Price or guide price.
- Timeline.
- Client responsibilities.
- Privacy note.
- Next step.

## Shared Proposal Language

### About Gen AI Solutions

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive business data to public cloud AI tools.

Based in Tralee, Co. Kerry and serving organisations across Ireland, the work focuses on private, offline, self-hosted and open-source AI systems for teams that handle sensitive documents, meetings and internal knowledge.

### Privacy Note

Please do not send sensitive files through email or a general contact form. If sensitive material needs to be reviewed, we will agree a safer review process first.

### Compliance Note

This work can support privacy-conscious AI decisions, safer staff practices and clearer internal AI rules. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee GDPR compliance. Legal, regulatory or sector-specific advice should be obtained from an appropriate adviser.

### Human Review Note

AI output should be reviewed by a person before it is used for client work, legal work, medical decisions, financial decisions, staff matters or other sensitive operations.

## Template 1: AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

### Proposal Title

AI Privacy and Readiness Audit

### Recommended For

[Client name] wants to explore AI but needs a practical first step before staff use public AI tools with sensitive documents, meetings or internal knowledge.

### Objectives

- Identify one or more useful AI use cases.
- Understand what sensitive data is involved.
- Review current staff AI use and workflow risks.
- Decide whether public, private, offline, self-hosted or open-source AI is suitable.
- Produce a practical action plan.

### What Is Included

- Short discovery session.
- Review of current AI use and intended use cases.
- High-level data sensitivity review.
- Workflow mapping for selected use cases.
- Practical recommendation on tools and setup options.
- AI safety and staff guidance notes.
- Written action plan.
- Follow-up call to discuss findings.

### What The Client Receives

- Summary of current AI opportunities and risks.
- Recommended first use case.
- Tool and deployment options.
- Suggested staff rules.
- Practical next steps.
- Clear recommendation on whether to build, train, test or wait.

### What Is Not Included

- Legal advice.
- GDPR compliance certification.
- Production AI build.
- Penetration testing.
- Review of large document sets unless separately agreed.
- Use of sensitive files through email or general forms.

### Guide Price

From €495.

Final pricing depends on the number of use cases, sessions and review depth.

### Timeline

Usually 1 to 2 weeks from kickoff, depending on availability and scope.

### Next Step

Approve the audit and schedule the discovery session.

## Red Lines

Do not include:

- Guaranteed GDPR compliance.
- Guaranteed cost savings.
- Fake case studies.
- Fake testimonials.
- Claims that AI output can be trusted without review.
- Broad promises to automate the business.
- Sensitive file transfer through general contact forms.

Do include:

- Privacy-conscious design.
- Human review.
- Clear scope.
- Plain-English deliverables.
- Practical next step.
- Limits and assumptions.
