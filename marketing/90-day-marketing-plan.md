# 90-Day Marketing Plan

## Aim

Generate real enquiries from Irish organisations that handle sensitive data and are cautious about public AI tools.

The offer stack should stay narrow:

- AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495.
- 20-minute AI privacy assessment.
- Private Document Assistant.
- Offline Meeting Intelligence.
- Offline AI Starter Setup.
- AI Safety and Staff Training.

The positioning is:

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive business data to public cloud AI tools.

## Commercial Priorities

The main goal is qualified conversations, not reach, likes or broad awareness.

Track:

- Direct replies from outreach.
- 20-minute assessment requests.
- Audit enquiries.
- Audit proposals sent.
- Audits won.
- Follow-on build opportunities from audits.
- Useful referral conversations.

Do not optimise for:

- Generic AI content views.
- Follower count without enquiry quality.
- Broad "AI automation" language.
- Viral posts that dilute privacy-first positioning.

## Target Markets

Start with sectors where privacy risk is easy to understand and the first offer is credible.

Accountants and bookkeepers handle client accounts, payroll, tax records, internal notes and financial documents. The strongest first angle is "what should staff not paste into public AI tools?"

Solicitors and legal offices handle confidential client files, contracts, correspondence, case notes and internal templates. The strongest first angle is "private AI for document search and safer staff use."

Schools, training providers and community organisations handle student, staff, learner, parent, volunteer and board information. The strongest first angle is "clear AI rules and private handling of policies, meetings and internal knowledge."

## Core Offer Ladder

The 20-minute AI privacy assessment is the entry point. It should identify one practical use case, confirm whether sensitive data is involved and decide whether an audit is worth discussing.

The AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495 is the first paid offer. It reviews use cases, data risks, current workflows, likely tools and a practical next step. It must not be described as legal advice or a guarantee of GDPR compliance.

The Offline AI Starter Setup is for a small team that wants a private ChatGPT-style assistant on local hardware or controlled infrastructure.

The Private Document Assistant is for organisations that want staff to ask questions across approved documents, policies, procedures, manuals or reports, with source references.

Offline Meeting Intelligence is for teams that want private transcription, summaries, action points and searchable records without uploading sensitive recordings to public platforms.

AI Safety and Staff Training is for teams already using public AI tools without clear rules.

## Message Pillars

Private AI for Irish businesses that handle sensitive data.

Public AI tools can be useful, but they are not always suitable for confidential business data.

Start with one private AI use case before buying tools or asking staff to experiment.

Open-source and self-hosted AI can give a business more control, transparency and flexibility when the use case suits it.

The safest first step is often a practical review of documents, workflows and staff use, not a large AI project.

## 90-Day Plan

### Days 1 to 14: Build the enquiry base

Create three prospect lists:

- Accountants and bookkeepers in Kerry, Cork, Limerick, Galway, Waterford and Dublin.
- Solicitors and small legal offices in Kerry, Cork, Limerick, Galway and Dublin.
- Schools, training providers, charities and community organisations in Kerry and nearby counties.

For each prospect, collect only business contact information that is publicly available and relevant. Do not scrape personal data unnecessarily.

Create a simple outreach tracker with:

- Organisation.
- Sector.
- Location.
- Contact person where publicly available.
- Email or LinkedIn profile.
- First message date.
- Follow-up dates.
- Response.
- Assessment booked or requested.
- Audit proposal sent.
- Outcome.

Prepare one short Loom-style demo using sample documents only:

- Private Document Assistant answering questions from example policies.
- Offline transcription turning a sample meeting into notes and actions.
- Local AI assistant running without public cloud upload.

Every demo must be labelled as a demo or example build.

### Days 15 to 30: Start direct outreach

Send a small batch every working day rather than a large blast.

Suggested daily cadence:

- 8 accountants or bookkeepers.
- 6 solicitors or legal offices.
- 6 schools, training providers or community organisations.
- 5 LinkedIn connection or follow-up messages.

Use plain-text emails. Keep them short. The aim is a reply or a requested assessment, not a full sale in the first message.

Primary CTA:

Would it be useful to do a 20-minute AI privacy assessment and identify one safe use case?

Secondary CTA:

I can also send the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit outline if that is more useful.

### Days 31 to 45: Tighten around replies

Review which sector replies most often and which angle creates the best conversations.

Adjust messaging based on real replies:

- If people mention staff already using ChatGPT, lead with AI Safety and Staff Training.
- If they mention file search, lead with Private Document Assistant.
- If they mention meetings, lead with Offline Meeting Intelligence.
- If they are unsure where to begin, lead with the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit.

Create a simple referral ask:

"Do you know another Irish business that is cautious about AI because of client data or internal documents?"

Use it only after a useful conversation, not in cold outreach.

### Days 46 to 60: Add local trust

Update Google Business Profile with service descriptions, practical posts and local photos where appropriate.

Ask real clients or contacts for honest reviews only where there has been a genuine business interaction. Do not script or invent reviews.

Prepare short posts around local business concerns:

- What Irish SMEs should decide before staff use public AI tools.
- What not to paste into ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Claude.
- How a private document assistant can help without uploading client files.
- Why the first step should be a small audit, not a large AI project.

### Days 61 to 75: Package the audit

Turn the AI Privacy and Readiness Audit into the main offer for outbound.

The audit should be easy to understand:

- Starts from €495.
- Reviews use cases, sensitive data risks and workflows.
- Produces a practical action plan.
- Can recommend public AI rules, private AI setup, staff training, or no build yet.

Create two simple proposal versions:

- Standard audit.
- Audit plus staff workshop.

Do not present the audit as compliance certification.

### Days 76 to 90: Double down on what converts

Rank enquiries by fit:

- Handles sensitive documents, meetings or internal knowledge.
- Has a clear use case.
- Has a real reason to avoid public cloud AI tools.
- Can pay for a €495 audit or larger setup.
- Has a decision-maker involved.

Stop doing channels that do not create replies or assessment requests.

Increase the best-performing outreach sequence by 50 percent, while keeping it personalised and relevant.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

Monday:

- Review previous week's replies.
- Select this week's prospect batch.
- Post one practical LinkedIn post.

Tuesday to Thursday:

- Send direct outreach.
- Follow up with previous contacts.
- Run assessment calls.

Friday:

- Send proposals.
- Update tracker.
- Record one short demo or answer a common question in public.

## Messaging Rules

Do not say:

- We do everything AI.
- Transform your business overnight.
- Guaranteed GDPR compliance.
- Fully secure in every scenario.
- Proven client results unless the result is real and approved for use.

Say:

- Start with one private AI use case.
- Do not send sensitive files through a general form.
- We can arrange a safer review process if sensitive material needs to be reviewed.
- The best option depends on data sensitivity, hardware, scale and support requirements.
- The audit is practical support, not legal advice.

## Minimum Viable Funnel

Outbound email or LinkedIn message.

20-minute AI privacy assessment.

AI Privacy and Readiness Audit from €495.

Practical recommendation.

Follow-on proposal for one of:

- Offline AI Starter Setup.
- Private Document Assistant.
- Offline Meeting Intelligence.
- AI Safety and Staff Training.

## 90-Day Success Criteria

By day 90, aim to have:

- A clean prospect list for three sectors.
- At least three tested outreach sequences.
- A repeatable assessment call script.
- A repeatable audit proposal.
- A short demo video using sample data.
- A clear view of which sector replies best.
- A small set of real questions and objections to inform the next website and content sprint.
