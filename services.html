Private and online AI, chosen around your business

Online tools can be useful for drafting, research, day-to-day tasks and connected workflows. Private, local and self-hosted systems can offer more control when documents, recordings or internal knowledge need a different approach. The starting point is the task, the people using it and what information may be shared.

Each proposal explains the tools, account controls, data handling, connections, costs and review responsibilities involved. A business subscription does not make every use suitable for sensitive information, and a local installation is not automatically an offline workflow.
