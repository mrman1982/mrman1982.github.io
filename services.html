Connect repetitive business tasks

Connect applications so staff spend less effort copying the same information between them. Use ordinary automation where a fixed rule is enough, and AI where interpreting or drafting information adds value.

Before choosing a service

Do we need a technical brief?

No. Describe the problem, who it affects and the systems you already use. For a website, include the public address. For AI, describe the input and the result you would like, without sending confidential examples.

How are projects and support priced?

A proposal sets out the work, handover and price after the requirement is reviewed. Hardware, licences, subscriptions and any ongoing support are identified so you can understand the full commitment.

How is business information handled?

Access and data handling are considered before connecting systems. Online provider controls vary by product and plan. Local or self-hosted processing can offer more control, with permissions and human checks still needed.

Can the first project be small?

Yes. A repair, a defined setup, staff workshop or limited pilot can stand on its own. The proposal explains what it delivers and what would be needed for any later expansion.
