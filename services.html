Additional capabilities

Broader AI work, kept in the right context

These services are still available where they support a sensible privacy-first business case. They are not the lead offer.

Model adaptation and fine-tuning

For specialist terminology or repeatable tasks where a standard model is not enough. This is considered only after the simpler private setup has been assessed.

Private media and image workflows

Local image or media workflows for teams that need to protect prompts, product ideas, internal assets or creative material.

Self-hosted media management

Private photo and video library options for organisations that want to organise media without relying on public cloud storage.

Consent-based voice workflows only

Voice replication or synthetic voice work is handled carefully, only with explicit consent, a clear legitimate use case and suitable controls. It is not used for impersonation.
