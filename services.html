Private AI for sensitive business information

Use AI with more control over your data

Public AI tools can be useful, but client files, contracts, financial records and meeting recordings need careful handling. Private AI can be designed to run on your own hardware or controlled infrastructure rather than sending that material to a public AI platform.

Each proposal explains where documents, prompts, outputs and backups are stored, which connections are needed and what staff must review. Offline operation depends on the agreed tools and setup. It is tested rather than assumed.
