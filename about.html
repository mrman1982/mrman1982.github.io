About Gen AI Solutions

Practical help with the technology your business relies on, from servers, websites and email to private and online AI.

Business systems that fit the way you work

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses set up and improve servers, websites, business email and everyday IT. The work starts with the problem you want to solve, the systems you already use and the level of support you need.

AI work can include online tools, integrations and automation, as well as local assistants, private document search and offline transcription. The right setup depends on the task, the information involved and how your team will use it. Training and clear handover help staff work confidently with the chosen tools.

A practical, privacy-conscious approach

Based in Tralee, Co. Kerry, Gen AI Solutions works with businesses and organisations across Ireland. You can start with a specific job, such as repairing a website, configuring domain email, setting up shared storage or putting an AI workflow to work.

The first step is to understand what is already in place. Recommendations explain the options, dependencies and ongoing costs in plain English before work begins. Any account subscriptions, licences or hardware requirements are agreed as part of the scope.

More control over sensitive information

For AI projects, the review considers what data is involved and where it will be processed. Online tools can suit many tasks. Local, offline and self-hosted options provide another route where greater control is needed. Provider settings, staff guidance and human review matter alongside the technical setup.
