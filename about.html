About Gen AI Solutions

Practical help with the technology your business relies on, alongside private AI for the information you need to protect.

Business systems that fit the way you work

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses set up and improve servers, websites, business email and everyday IT. The work starts with the problem you want to solve, the systems you already use and the level of support you need.

Private AI is a core part of that offer. Document assistants, local ChatGPT-style tools and offline transcription can help teams use internal knowledge while keeping sensitive material on their own hardware or agreed, controlled infrastructure.

A practical, privacy-conscious approach

Based in Tralee, Co. Kerry, Gen AI Solutions works with businesses and organisations across Ireland. You can start with a specific job, such as repairing a website, configuring domain email, setting up shared storage or trying one private AI use case.

The first step is to understand what is already in place. Recommendations explain the options, dependencies and ongoing costs in plain English before work begins. Any account subscriptions, licences or hardware requirements are agreed as part of the scope.

More control over sensitive information

For AI projects, the review considers what data is involved and where it will be processed. Offline, local, self-hosted and open-source tools are considered where they make sense. Staff guidance and human review matter alongside the technical setup.
