About Gen AI Solutions

Our Mission

Gen AI Solutions helps Irish businesses use AI privately, safely and practically, without sending sensitive business data to public cloud AI tools. The work is focused on private document assistants, offline transcription, local AI assistants, staff training and bespoke offline workflows.

Our Experience

Based in Tralee, Co. Kerry, the business serves organisations across Ireland that handle client files, internal documents, policies, recordings or confidential knowledge. The approach is to start small, choose one useful use case and build only where a private, offline or self-hosted setup makes practical sense.

Practical Offline Capabilities

We focus on privacy, local control and sustainable performance rather than raw hardware specs. The offline AI approach combines sensible model selection, private document search, local transcription and clear handover notes so teams understand what they can use safely.

Why Choose Us?

  • Practical starting points: Work begins with one useful use case, not a vague promise to automate everything.
  • Privacy first: Offline, local and self-hosted options are considered when a business needs more control over sensitive data.
  • Plain-English advice: Recommendations explain the trade-offs clearly so business owners can make informed decisions.
  • Training and handover: Staff guidance, safe-use rules and clear handover notes are part of the work where needed.
